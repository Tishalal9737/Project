OUR MISSION

Providing innovative and sustainable technological solutions to help companies and entities address the challenges of the 21st century in the fields of energy, sustainability,space, and defense. We are committed to guiding our clients and partners through technological transformation, offering strategic consulting and practical assistance toimplement cutting-edge solutions that enhance their efficiency and competitiveness. We promote innovation, sustainability, and social responsibility, and view partnerships as a key tool for the necessary evolution to respond to external changes.

OUR SKILLS

STAM’s system engineering expertise cover all phases of the project life cycle, driving efficiency and innovative solutions across diverse domains. We develop tailored digital solutions and master proprietary Open Innovation tools, fostering collaboration and meeting specific industry needs. Actively contributing to the strategic roadmaps of several European Technology Platforms, we leverage ESA’s Technology Transfer Programme and EU funding instruments to pioneer impactful innovations, guiding projects from concept to validation with cutting-edge engineering tools.

BUSINESS AREAS

Energy & Environment

STAM is involved in all levels of research with respect to sustainable and environmental design for spaces, buildings, neighborhoods and cities.

Industry & Automation

STAM has been specialising in the design and manufacturing of innovative mechatronic systems for industrial automation.

Security & Transport

A large part of STAM activities is related to safety and security aspects of critical infrastructures in transportation engineering.

Space & Defence

Stam experience builds on 20 years of support to big players in the space and defence fields, where engineering services were tailored to the needs of long term national and international programmes.

Open Innovation

Stam builds-up and animates open-innovation ecosystems, leveraging on proprietary methodologies and IT platforms. We connect clients and partners to assets and opportunities generated across a network of more than 500 organisations.

R&D Services

Stam has over 20 years of experience in supporting innovators to conceive and design new products and services which leverage upon our multidisciplinary experts, in-house laboratories, hands-on integration of proven methodologies and modelling tools.
